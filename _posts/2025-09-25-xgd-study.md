# Chaos vs Control: Using Principal Component Analysis and K-means Clustering to Identify Styles.

![3d chart](/images/chaos.png)
Read my long blog post [here](https://databetweenthelines.substack.com/p/chaos-vs-control).

This project (find here on [GitHub](https://github.com/louis-porter/between-the-lines/tree/main/articles/chaos-maxing)) uses inspiration from The Double-Pivot podcast as well as Andoni Iraola to explore the idea of chaos-maxing in modern football. The meta team style has began to shift away from the slow and measured approach popularised by Pep Guardiola as we see teams with less resources exceed expectations by playing incredibly wide, direct and aggressive.

I used data science techniques to group team styles together using unsupervised methods such as PCA and K-means clustering.
