# Predicting Player Wages: A Machine Learning Approach Using FM24 Data

![Most over/underpaid players in the premier league](/images/wages.png)
This project (find here on [GitHub](https://github.com/louis-porter/football-analytics/tree/main/models/wage-prediction-model)) uses Python to create a machine learning model that estimates player wages. The study used an XGBoost model to predict player wages based on various factors including technical abilities, physical attributes, age, and club. Key findings include that Premier League membership is the strongest predictor of wages, and mental attributes like Anticipation are surprisingly important. The model achieved 79.4% accuracy (R² score) but showed some limitations, particularly with loan players and recent Saudi Pro League disruptions. The study found that the median footballer wage (£2,900/week) is nearly 4 times the UK median wage, and highlighted some notably overpaid players like Jadon Sancho and Christian Eriksen. The total annual wages for the 16,250 players studied amounted to approximately £9.6 billion.
